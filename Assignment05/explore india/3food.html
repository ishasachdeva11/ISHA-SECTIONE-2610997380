Famous Indian Foods

Indian cuisine is one of the most diverse and enjoyable cuisine by Indians.It is very diverse because different regions of india have different ingredients,species,and cooking methods.Even people outside india loves to taste the indian dishes.In this we have different types of cuisine-north indian,south indian,west indian ,east.It is known for its rich flavours, colourful spices and variety of dishes. Every region of India has its own special food. North India is famous for dishes like naan, butter chicken and rajma, while South India is known for dosa, idli and sambar. Gujarat is popular for dhokla, and Punjab is famous for sarson da saag and makki di roti. Indian food uses spices such as turmeric, cumin, cardamom and cloves, which give it a unique taste and aroma. Masala Dosa
Masala dosa is a famous South Indian dish made from fermented rice and lentil batter. It is a thin, crispy pancake filled with spicy potato masala. It is generally served with coconut chutney and sambar. It is a tasty and popular breakfast dish.
Chole Bhature
Chole Bhature is a popular North Indian dish. It consists of spicy chickpea curry called chole and soft, fluffy fried bread called bhature. It is often served with onions, pickle and chutney. It is a favourite street food and is enjoyed by people of all ages.
Dhokla
Dhokla is a popular Gujarati dish made from fermented gram flour batter. It is soft, light and slightly spongy in texture. It is usually steamed and served with green chutney. Dhokla is commonly eaten as a snack or breakfast.
