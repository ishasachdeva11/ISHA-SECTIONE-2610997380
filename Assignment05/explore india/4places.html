FAMOUS PLACES IN INDIA


Jaipur - Rajasthan
Jaipur is known as the Pink City. It is famous for its beautiful forts, palaces, colourful markets and rich Rajasthani culture>

Varanasi – Uttar Pradesh
Varanasi is one of the oldest cities in India. It is famous for the River Ganga, ancient temples and evening Ganga Aarti

Amritsar – Punjab
Amritsar is famous for the Golden Temple, one of the most important and peaceful religious places in India. The city is also known for Jallianwala Bagh.

Jammu and Kashmir
Jammu and Kashmir is famous for its snow-covered mountains, beautiful valleys, lakes and gardens. It is often called “Paradise on Earth.”
