WELCOME TO EXPLORE INDIA

**India as a Travel Destination**

India is one of the most beautiful and diverse travel destinations in the world. It is famous for its rich history, colourful culture, delicious food and beautiful landscapes. Tourists can visit the majestic Taj Mahal in Agra, the forts and palaces of Rajasthan, the peaceful backwaters of Kerala and the scenic mountains of Himachal Pradesh and Kashmir. India also offers beautiful beaches, wildlife sanctuaries and many famous religious places. Every region has its own traditions, festivals, clothes and cuisine, making India unique and exciting. The warm hospitality of Indian people makes visitors feel welcome. Thus, India is an ideal travel destination for people who want to experience history, nature, culture and adventure all in one country.
