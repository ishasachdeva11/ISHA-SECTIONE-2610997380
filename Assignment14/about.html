About Chitkara

One of India's top-rated private universities, Chitkara University is known for shaping industry-ready leaders through innovation, research and world-class education. Established in 2002 and recognised under Sections 2(f) and 22(1) of the UGC Act, Chitkara University stands as one of the best private universities in North India, shaping industry-ready leaders through innovation, research and world‑class education. The sprawling campus sits 30 kilometres from Chandigarh on the Chandigarh‑Patiala National Highway, equidistant from Mohali, Panchkula, Ambala and Patiala. The University offers multi-disciplinary programs, from computer science, electronics and mechanical engineering to business management, nursing, hotel management and architecture, all designed to be industry-relevant. A strong academic heritage and firmness on principles has seen it grow from strength to strength. In 2010, the Punjab State Legislature passed "The Chitkara University Act," cementing its status as a recognised university.
